### Dahlia Universe

> Live stream clips are productive re-creations that transform long-form content into standalone assets, driving their own viewers, engagement, and traffic. Impactful clips pull audiences back to the original stream, extending content lifespan and sustaining continuous growth.
> This space develops tools and workflows designed to efficiently capture, process, and convert live moments into shareable value. Editing, refining, and redistributing key moments is a vital creative act that sustains creators and expands communities.
> The utilities, fan projects, and workflows presented here offer experiments, tests, and solutions to scale and evolve the clip-based ecosystem.
