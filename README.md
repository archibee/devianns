### Dahlia Universe

> Live stream clips transform long-form broadcasts into standalone assets that drive fresh views, engagement, and traffic. By steering audiences back to the original stream, impactful clips extend content lifespan and power sustained growth. This space develops tools and workflows to seamlessly capture, edit, and share highlight moments. Refining and redistributing key scenes is an essential creative act that sustains creators and expands communities. Through our utilities, fan projects, and experimental workflows, we offer real solutions to scale the clip ecosystem.
